Pick a place in the bar (or press 1 to 6), or click a temple, the tank or anyone, for a close-up; Whole island (or Esc) goes back. Close in on a temple, the tank or any lane and the houses around sink to stubs so you can see in. Drag to move around the island; shift-drag (or right-drag, or the turn buttons) to turn it. Everything is at one scale, 1 world unit to 4 m, except that the landmarks are drawn larger than life so they stand out from the houses (Uma Maheshwar, Bagh Bhairab and Chilancho by about a third, Lohan Dega by nearly half), and people are drawn larger so you can see them. Turn on Measure and click two points for the straight distance, the walking distance along the mapped lanes, and the climb between them. With Measure off, click the ground for its height above sea level. People walk the real lane network to real places; click anyone to see where they are going and how far they have walked.
